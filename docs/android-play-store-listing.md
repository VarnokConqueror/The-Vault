## Full description

The Vault is a private Android messaging app built for direct chats, group conversations, voice notes, calling, and original-quality attachments.

Keep your people close without turning every conversation into a noisy feed. The Vault focuses on the core things that matter: sending messages fast, sharing media without forced compression by default, and keeping privacy controls easy to find.

What you can do in The Vault:

- Send direct messages and group messages
- Share photos, videos, and files in original quality by default
- Record and send voice notes
- Start voice calls
- Use stickers and device emoji
- Add contacts by ID or QR
- Protect the app with PIN and biometric lock
- Read the Privacy Policy and Terms inside the app
- Send anonymous in-app feedback without leaving the app

Built for the Android rollout:

- Notification controls and in-app privacy settings
- Attachment handling that keeps the original file unless you choose otherwise
- Fast chat, media, and feedback flows built around the current live relay

The Vault is actively being refined during rollout, and the Android experience will continue improving as testing expands.
